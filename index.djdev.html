Phase 01  /  Warm-up · 23:00

THE SET ARCHITECTURE

Live set philosophy — No pre-recorded sequences, no fixed setlists. Every frequency is shaped live in the booth, reading the pulse of the room from cold doors to full sunrise.

Every night is a circuit. The doors open. The room is cold. I warm the wire, 118, 120, 122, until the floor starts moving on its own. Then we climb. 124. 126. 128. Peak isn't a moment. It's a decision the whole room makes at once. After 3AM the rules dissolve. Hypnotic. Open-ended. No plan survives the sunrise. The set follows the room. The room follows the current. I just hold the wire.

— DEV, Maharashtra, India
